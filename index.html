CALIBRATION

Step 1 — Tare (Zero the scale)

Remove everything from the platform, then press Tare. This tares BOTH the ESP32's own baseline and the Pi's calibration offset — same as the on-screen TARE button and what happens automatically at startup.

Step 2 — Calibrate with known weight

After taring, place your reference weight on the platform and enter its value below.

Manual Calibration Values (Advanced)

Edit raw offsets and factors directly. Offsets are in raw ESP32 counts (post ESP32-side tare), factors convert raw counts to kg. Only change if you know what you're doing.
